How CounterPay works

Two ways to pay. Members are cash patients in both.

CounterPay is a TPA-backed payments product that unlocks cash pay, backed by your self-insured plan. Members carry a debit card and an app. Providers see a self-pay patient and get paid like one.

Routine care

Pay at the register

Pay at the register with your CounterPay Card. Upload the superbill. Done.

  1. Find careThe app or a nurse points to a high-quality provider, often at $0 to the member.
  2. Check in as self-payNo insurance card and no claim. Scheduled care starts with a price agreed in advance.
  3. Pay with your CounterPay CardTap the card at the register, backed by your employer's plan.
  4. Upload the superbillSnap it in the app. Plan rules are checked and your plan records update.
Paid at the register. Done.
Emergency care

We settle the bill

Submit the superbill to CounterPay. Our bill negotiation team settles the bill with the provider within 30 days.

  1. Get care anywhereThe member registers as self-pay. No network to check.
  2. Submit the superbillUpload it in the app, or the provider sends it with your CounterPay number.
  3. Our team negotiatesOur bill negotiation team audits every line and agrees a fair price in writing.
  4. Bill paidYou approve the agreed price and your share in the app, and we pay the provider.

$0 Steering

Choose a recommended provider and pay $0.

In the app, members find high-quality providers at fair cash prices. When they pick one of those instead of the ER or a hospital outpatient department, CounterPay sets their cost to $0. The member saves, and the plan saves far more.

  • Shown before they go. "$0 for you" appears in care search and comes from the nurse line.
  • Applied automatically. At a recommended provider, the member's share is set to $0 when the receipt is checked.
  • Steered where it matters. Orthopedic walk-in clinics and urgent care instead of the ER, plus imaging, outpatient surgery and labs, where prices vary most for the same service.

Where it matters

Urgent needs and planned care

Many ER visits don't need an ER. Members get the same care faster at a walk-in clinic, and planned care costs far less outside the hospital.

  • Orthopedic walk-in clinics for sprains, strains and broken bones
  • Urgent care for non-emergencies
  • Imaging at independent centers
  • Outpatient surgery at surgery centers
  • Labs at independent labs
Why it pays for itself

The plan saves far more than it waives

Moving a procedure from a hospital outpatient department to a surgery center saves about 40% of its cost. The member's share the plan covers is a small part of that.

HSA plans

Works with high-deductible plans

In HSA-qualified high-deductible plans, $0 applies once the deductible is met. Before that, employers can reward the choice with an HSA contribution.
